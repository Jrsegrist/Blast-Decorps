# Regions compare.py skips: they legitimately differ between the emulator and
# bc_headless.exe.  `LO HI reason` (hex, [LO, HI)) or `SYMBOL [SIZE] reason`.
# Also skipped automatically (compare.py load_ignore): the .data/.bss of the
# libultra objects the platform layer replaces, and the hd_code text range
# except the tables pinned there (the .nm_pin_* sections of the NM ELF).

# --- graphics
80000400 8004B400 the two 320x240 16-bit framebuffers gFrameBuffers (nothing renders natively)
8004B400 80055400 in levels the RSP output buffer (gRdpOutputBuffer, 0xA000: the cull test's RDP output); before, the decompressors' scratch (inflate tables, size-probe copies)
8021ED00 80244000 the Z-buffer (cleared by the RDP); also the staging area of compressed loads (port_assets.c)

# --- audio
# The audio heap gAudioHeapBuf (0x2A280 bytes, 1C460.c music_init_audio) holds the
# banks and the sequence table (compared), then the synthesizer, the
# sequence player and the sound player.  Natively the synthesizer comes from
# ultralib's newer libaudio (synthesizer/drvrnew/reverb/env are still the old
# SDK's hand asm in the ROM), whose allocations differ by 0x10-0x40 bytes,
# so everything allocated after the sequence table is shifted.
80370C80 8037C720 audio heap: the music and effects banks (libaudio rounds an ADPCM wavetable's length to whole frames when a voice first plays it: audio timing)
8037C720 8039AF00 audio heap after the sequence table: synthesizer (newer SDK natively), sequence + sound players

80368070 80368308 audio manager (22EE0.c __am): Acmd list / AudioInfo pointers into the audio heap, its queues and OSThread
gAudCmdLen 4 Acmd count of the last audio frame (the native synthesizer is ultralib's)
# The audio thread's bookkeeping follows the RSP: natively a frame's gfx task
# holds the RSP until its RDP finishes (no yield to audio tasks), so audio
# frames run a retrace later than in the emulator.  Its state (buffer ring,
# AudioInfo, Acmd lists, queues) and the sound player's lists, which point
# into the shifted audio heap, are not compared.
802F3AF0 802F3B00 audio manager .data (22EE0.c): buffer ring indices
8036A300 8036AFB0 audio manager .bss (22EE0.c): AudioInfo records, Acmd lists, message queues
80367518 80367708 the music sequences' read state (ALCSeq gSeqs[]): advanced by the sequence player in audio time
gSndPlayer 60 the sound player (1A630.c): ALPlayer timing, event queue, lists into the audio heap
802E8CE0 802E8CF4 the sound player's active/free lists and its active sound count gSndVoiceCount (1A630.c .data)
803F7844 803F7848 a vehicle's special-move sound handle (8AEE0.c): which sound-player slot sndPlaySfx hands out follows the audio thread's timing (the game only tests it for NULL)
